03 / EMULATION REALITY CHECK

Why QEMU-in-WebAssembly won't boot this IPSW

QEMU's generic ARM virtual machines do not reproduce the iPhone 11's Apple SoC, Secure Boot chain, device tree, SEP, storage controller, display and other platform hardware. An IPSW is an Apple restore bundle—not a standalone virtual-machine disk.
